LCD FINAL WIRING  (decided 2026-10-27; replaces the pull-up / divider / RC experiments)
=====================================================================================

RULE: no LCD input may be fed 5 V, and every LCD input must be LOW while the module
powers up. High = ~3.3-3.4 V from a DRIVEN logic output (like the ESP32 GPIOs that run
this module fine), never 5 V through a resistor.

Why (from measurements):
  - RST clamped at 2.3-2.5 V through both 10k and 1k pull-ups, and forcing RST to 5 V
    killed the backlight; the backlight flashed as power went off; replugging fixed it.
    = the module was being back-powered through its input-protection diodes, which keeps
    its on-board 3.3 V regulator from starting cleanly.
  - The same module is stable on the ESP32: 3.3 V push-pull pins, low/floating at power-up.
  So: pull-ups to +5V, RST/EN tied to +5V and dividers from 5 V all made it worse.

LCD pin   Connect to                              Level
-------   --------------------------------------  -----------------------------------------
VCC       +5V  (10uF + 100nF at the module)        module makes its own 3.3 V
GND       GND
SDA       LCD latch (74LS161) pin 14, direct       0 / ~3.4 V
SCL       LCD latch pin 13, direct                 0 / ~3.4 V
DC        LCD latch pin 12, direct                 0 / ~3.4 V
CS        LCD latch pin 11, direct                 0 / ~3.4 V
RST       oscillator 74LS04 pin 4                  low during CPU reset, ~3.4 V after
EN        oscillator 74LS04 pin 4 (same as RST)    low during CPU reset, ~3.4 V after
TE        not connected

NO pull-ups, NO series resistors, NO dividers, NO RC parts on any LCD pin.

Oscillator 74LS04 spare inverters (the crystal uses the other side):
  pin 1 <- CPU pin 40 (/RES)   pin 2 -> pin 3   pin 4 -> LCD RST and LCD EN   pin 5 -> GND

Power-up sequence this gives (same as the ESP32 driver):
  /RES low (power-on RC on CPU pin 40) -> latch cleared: SDA/SCL/DC/CS = 0, RST/EN = 0
  -> module powers up with nothing feeding its pins -> /RES rises -> RST/EN go high
  -> firmware waits, then SWRESET, SLPOUT, ... DISPON (flappy_bb.bin, ESP32 command list).
Recommended: 10uF (instead of 1uF) from CPU pin 40 to GND -> ~100 ms power-on reset.

Program: build/flappy_bb.bin (SCL toggled in software on latch pin 13).

LATCH CLOCK FIX (2026-10-27): with the boards split, the write strobe (decoder pin 11 ->
LCD latch pin 2) runs over a long wire and rings; the extra edge re-clocked the latch after
the data had gone, loading 1111 (SDA/SCL/DC/CS stuck high, blank screen). Touching pin 2
with a meter probe (~100 pF) made it work. Fix: 10 pF ceramic ("100") from latch pin 2 to
GND at the latch (plus optional 100R in series at the decoder end). Now draws on its own.
RESULT: flappy_bb.bin draws the game with this wiring.
