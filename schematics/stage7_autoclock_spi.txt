STAGE 7 - AUTO-CLOCK SPI  (74LS00 + 74LS04, both on hand)  -> needed by flappy.bin v2
=======================================================================================

Idea: keep the U5 latch for SDA / DC / CS, but stop toggling SCL in software.
A write to $A000-$BFFF makes a short SCL pulse BY ITSELF (during that write cycle).
The panel samples on the pulse's rising edge, when the latch still holds the value of
the PREVIOUS write; at the end of the cycle the latch takes the new value. So:
one 4-cycle store = clock out one bit and set up the next. Pixels go ~3x faster.

  $8000-$9FFF  write: latch only (no clock)         (A13 = 0)
  $A000-$BFFF  write: SCL pulse + latch             (A13 = 1)

   SCL = IOSEL AND A13 AND write-strobe   (high only during phi2 of a write to $A000)


ONE NEW CHIP: U13 74LS00 (4 NANDs, two used as inverters) + 2 spare inverters on U9
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------
U9 (the oscillator 74LS04) has 3 spare inverters whose inputs were tied to GND:
  U9 pin 9  : REMOVE from GND -> U4 pin 6  (/IOSEL)     U9 pin 8  = IOSEL
  U9 pin 11 : REMOVE from GND -> U4 pin 11 (/WSTB)      U9 pin 10 = WSTB
  U9 pin 13 : stays on GND

U13 74LS00   (+5V pin 14, GND pin 7, 100nF across)
  G1  1 <- U9 pin 8 (IOSEL)    2 <- CPU pin 23 (A13)   3 = /SPISEL -> U13 4 and 5
  G2  4,5 <- U13 pin 3                                 6 = SPISEL  -> U13 9
  G3  9 <- U13 pin 6           10 <- U9 pin 10 (WSTB)  8 = /SCLK   -> U13 12 and 13
  G4  12,13 <- U13 pin 8                               11 = SCL    -> LCD SCL

  /IOSEL -[U9 9>8]-+
                   G1 -- G2 --+
  A13 -------------+          G3 -- G4 --> LCD SCL
  /WSTB -[U9 11>10]-----------+

LCD rewiring
  SCL : REMOVE the wire from U5 pin 13 (QB). Connect LCD SCL to U13 pin 11.
        Keep the 4.7k pull-up on the SCL line.
  SDA : U5 pin 14 (QA)  - unchanged
  DC  : U5 pin 12 (QC)  - unchanged
  CS  : U5 pin 11 (QD)  - unchanged

Nothing else changes: U5 still loads on every write to $8000-$BFFF (/LOAD = U4 pin 6,
CLK = U4 pin 11). A13 (CPU 23) already goes to SRAM pin 26; this is one more wire on it
(use a hub strip if the CPU pin is full).

CHECK (power off): U9 9 - U4 6; U9 11 - U4 11; U9 8 - U13 1; U13 2 - CPU 23;
U13 3 - U13 4 - U13 5; U13 6 - U13 9; U9 10 - U13 10; U13 8 - U13 12 - U13 13;
U13 11 - LCD SCL. U9 9 and U9 11 must NOT beep GND. LCD SCL must NOT beep U5 pin 13.

Idle state: SCL sits LOW (U13 pin 11 ~0 V) except during writes to $A000.
