STAGE 4 - LCD  (SmartElex 2" IPS, LHS200KP-IF05, ST7789P3, 240x320, 4-wire SPI)
=================================================================================

Datasheet: SmartElex-2-inch-IPS-TFT-LCD-module.pdf. Its reference wiring runs the module
straight off an Arduino Uno's 5 V and 5 V logic pins, so it connects directly to the latch.

First REMOVE the 4 test LEDs (and their resistors) from U5 QA-QD.

LCD pin   Connects to                          Function
-------   ----------------------------------   ------------------------------------------
VCC       +5V rail                             module power (on-board regulator)
GND       GND rail
EN        +5V rail                             backlight enable (always on)
DC        U5 pin 12 (QC)                       0 = command, 1 = data
SCL       U5 pin 13 (QB)                       SPI clock
CS        U5 pin 11 (QD)                       chip select, low = selected
TE        not connected                        tearing output, not used
RST       +5V rail (direct)                    held high; the program sends SWRESET.
                                               NOT to CPU /RES: the module's input load
                                               dragged /RES to 2.3 V (display stuck in reset)
SDA       U5 pin 14 (QA)                       SPI data (MOSI)

Latch bits at $8000:  bit0 QA = SDA   bit1 QB = SCL   bit2 QC = DC   bit3 QD = CS

Pull-ups: 4.7k from each of U5 pins 11-14 to +5V (raises the LS high level to ~5 V).

Keep the LCD wires short-ish (under ~15 cm) and add a 100nF + 10uF across the LCD's VCC/GND
near the module: the backlight draws ~20-60 mA.


TEST PROGRAM: build/lcdtest.bin   (source asm/lcdtest.s)
--------------------------------------------------------
./flash build/lcdtest.bin, put the EEPROM back, power on, press reset. At 625 kHz:

  0 - ~1 s    backlight on; init commands are being sent
  ~1 s        screen shows random noise  -> the display is on, init WORKED
  ~1 - 18 s   black wipes down the screen from the top  -> pixel writes work
  ~18 - 24 s  five 48x48 squares in the middle, left to right:
              RED  GREEN  BLUE  WHITE  YELLOW
  then        CPU halts, the picture stays

Simulated (make test): exact command bytes 01 11 3A[55] 36[00] 21 13 29, then
2A[00 00 00 EF] 2B[00 00 01 3F] 2C + 153600 zero bytes, then the 5 squares; every
reset/sleep delay is >= 120 ms (263 ms measured).


TROUBLESHOOTING
---------------
 Backlight off .................. EN or VCC not on +5V.
 Backlight on, never any noise .. init not received: check SDA/SCL/DC/CS go to the right
                                  U5 pins (easy to swap SDA and SCL), and RST = +5V.
                                  Measure U5 QB (SCL) during the first second: it should
                                  sit between 0 and 3 V (toggling).
 Noise but no black wipe ........ pixel data not arriving; same pin checks, DC especially.
 Colours wrong:
   black/white swapped, all colours inverted -> in asm/lcdtest.s change $21 (INVON) to $20.
   red and blue swapped                       -> change MADCTL data $00 to $08.
 Squares in the wrong place / sideways ....... note what you see; it's MADCTL orientation.


RESULT 2026-10-23: PASSED (lcdtest.bin, CRC 69F0). Noise -> black wipe -> five squares.
  The display really works on the R6502 through the 74LS161 latch with bit-banged SPI.
  The final fault was loose breadboard contacts (again). The latch outputs have 4.7k pull-ups;
  LCD RST is on +5V with SWRESET in software.

UPDATE 2026-10-23: landscape 320x240 (MADCTL $60) and a 1.25 MHz clock (U6 QC -> CPU 37).
  Shared driver: asm/lcd.inc (init, window, fast fill ~13 cycles/bit, 12x12 sprite).
  Graphics: tools/gfx.py -> asm/gfx.inc (palette, bird sprite) + build/bird.png preview.
  lcdtest.bin: black screen, then RED GREEN BLUE WHITE YELLOW squares left to right (~17 s).
  gfxtest.bin: sky, pipe pair with caps and shading, ground, and the bird (~16 s).
  `make test` renders both screens to build/lcdtest_screen.png and build/gfxtest_screen.png.
  If the picture is upside down, change MADCTL $60 to $A0 in asm/lcd.inc.
