FULL PIN MAP - everything wired so far (stage 2: clock + CPU + ROM + decoder + latch)
=====================================================================================

Every pin of every chip is listed. "n/c" = nothing connected.
Check continuity with the power OFF, probing chip pins (not breadboard holes).

Chips:
  CPU = R6502P       (DIP-40)  top board
  ROM = 28C64        (DIP-28)  top board
  U4  = 74LS00       (DIP-14)  top board     address decoder
  U5  = 74LS161      (DIP-16)  bottom board  LCD latch (test LED for now)
  U6  = 74LS161      (DIP-16)  bottom board  clock divider
  U9  = 74LS04       (DIP-14)  bottom board  crystal oscillator
  X1  = 10 MHz crystal
  SRAM: not wired yet. If it is on the board, only its /CS (pin 20) to +5V.
