R6502P (NMOS 6502, DIP-40) - CONNECTIONS
==========================================

Two columns:
  NOP TEST = CPU alone, data bus forced to $EA (NOP) through resistors. No ROM/RAM yet.
  FINAL    = full system (ROM, SRAM, 74LS00 decoder U4, LCD latch U5). Do this later.

Pin 1 is at the notch, top-left. Pins count down the left side (1-20), then up the right (21-40).

                    +----\/----+
    VSS (GND)   1 --|          |-- 40  /RES
    RDY         2 --|          |-- 39  phi2 out
    phi1 out    3 --|          |-- 38  SO
    /IRQ        4 --|          |-- 37  phi0 in
    NC          5 --|          |-- 36  NC

ROM = 28C64 (U2), SRAM = HY62CT08081E (U3), U4 = 74LS00 decoder, U5 = 74LS161 LCD latch,
U6 = 74LS161 clock divider. [n] = pin number on that chip.


NOP TEST: WHAT TO EXPECT
------------------------
D7..D0 = 1110 1010 = $EA = NOP. The CPU reads NOP from every address, so it runs NOPs
forever and the address counts up by 1 every 2 clocks.

  625 kHz / 2 cycles = 312,500 addresses per second
  A15 has a period of 65,536 addresses = 131,072 cycles -> 625,000 / 131,072 = ~4.8 Hz

  Blink rates (one on+off cycle) at 625 kHz:
    A15 ~4.8 Hz    A14 ~9.5 Hz    A13 ~19 Hz (looks like a flicker)

Steps:
 1. Power on, press and release /RES.
 2. A15 LED should blink steadily at ~4.8 Hz.
 3. No blink? Check pin 1 = GND (not +5V!), pin 37 has the clock, /RES is high after
    release, RDY is high, and the CPU gets 5 V at pin 8.

RESULT 2026-10-22: PASSED. A15 blinks at ~4.8 Hz with 10k resistors on D0-D7.
  - Powering from a TP4056 (Li-ion charger) sagged the rail to ~2.5 V. Use a regulated 5 V
    supply (4.75-5.25 V). The Arduino 5 V pin was fine for this test.
  - An NMOS 6502 output can't drive an LED brightly; buffer it through a spare 74LS04
    inverter (LED from +5V via 470R into the inverter output). That makes the LED inverted.

Why resistors on D0-D7 and not wires: during reset and on some cycles the CPU drives the
data bus itself. 4.7k lets it do that safely while still presenting $EA when it reads.
