FLAPPY6502 - ALL CONNECTIONS OF THE WORKING BUILD, STAGE BY STAGE
=================================================================
This file replaces the older stage notes. full_pinmap.txt is the stage-2 state, and
board_link.txt shows the abandoned stage-7 SCL gate and LCD pull-ups.
Matches hardware/flappy6502_schematic.pdf (netlist-checked).
Pin numbers are IC pin numbers (DIP: pin 1 at the notch / dot, counted down the left
side, then up the right). "n/c" = nothing connected.

CHIPS
-----
  U1  R6502P       DIP-40   CPU (NMOS 6502)
  U2  28C64        DIP-28   EEPROM 8 KiB, at $E000-$FFFF (also seen at $C000-$DFFF)
  U3  HY62CT08081E DIP-28   SRAM 32 KiB, at $0000-$7FFF (62256 pinout)
  U4  74LS00       DIP-14   address decoder (4 NAND gates)
  U5  74LS161      DIP-16   LCD latch / output port, at $8000-$BFFF
  U6  74LS161      DIP-16   clock divider (10 MHz -> CPU clock)
  U7  74LS04       DIP-14   crystal oscillator (gates 4-6) + LCD reset buffer (gates 1-2)
  Y1  10 MHz crystal (2 legs, no polarity)
  J1  2" ST7789 SPI LCD module (SmartElex LHS200KP-IF05): VCC GND SDA SCL DC CS RST EN TE
  SW1 reset button     SW2 flap button

Memory map: RAM $0000-$7FFF | LCD latch $8000-$BFFF | EEPROM $C000-$FFFF (code at $E000)


=================================================================================
STAGE 1 - POWER
=================================================================================
  5 V 2 A adapter -> TP4056 board IN+ / IN- pads -> +5V and GND rails.
  470uF (C7) + 10uF (C8) + 10uF (C5) + 100nF (C6) across +5V / GND at the input.
  Join the +5V rails and the GND rails on BOTH sides of BOTH breadboards.
  Every chip: a 100nF ceramic from its VCC pin to its GND pin, right at the chip.

  Chip    VCC (+5V)   GND
  U1      8           1, 21
  U2      28          14
  U3      28          14
  U4      14          7
  U5      16          8
  U6      16          8
  U7      14          7

  CHECK: +5V to GND must NOT beep. Powered: 4.75-5.25 V at every VCC pin.


=================================================================================
STAGE 2 - CLOCK: 74LS04 (U7) OSCILLATOR + 74LS161 (U6) DIVIDER
=================================================================================

U7 74LS04 is split in two halves:
  pins 8-13 (right side, inverters 4-6) = crystal oscillator   <- this stage
  pins 1-6  (left side,  inverters 1-3) = LCD reset buffer      -> stage 9
  pin 14 = +5V, pin 7 = GND, shared.

CRYSTAL OSCILLATOR (as built): inverters 6 and 5 oscillate, inverter 4 buffers
  Crystal Y1 10 MHz: one leg on pin 13, the other leg on pin 9 (no polarity)
  Jumper:            pin 10 to pin 9
  R1 560R:           pin 13 to pin 12
  C1 100nF:          pin 12 to pin 11
  R2 560R:           pin 11 to pin 10
  Output:            pin 8 -> U6 pin 2 (10 MHz)

                R1 560R                     R2 560R
         +------/\/\/------+         +------/\/\/------+
         |                 |         |                 |
 pin 13 -+--[ inv 6 ]>o----+--||-----+--[ inv 5 ]>o----+-- pin 10 = pin 9 --[ inv 4 ]>o-- pin 8 -> 10 MHz
  (6A)   |         pin 12 (6Y)  C1  pin 11 (5A)        |   (jumper)  (4A)        (4Y)   to U6 pin 2
         |                    100nF                    |
         +---------------------|[ Y1 ]|----------------+
                              10 MHz crystal

U7 pin table, oscillator side
  Pin  Name  Connects to
  13   6A    Y1 leg 1, R1 to pin 12
  12   6Y    R1 to pin 13, C1 to pin 11
  11   5A    C1 from pin 12, R2 to pin 10
  10   5Y    R2 to pin 11, jumper to pin 9
   9   4A    Y1 leg 2, jumper from pin 10
   8   4Y    U6 2  (10 MHz clock out)
  14   VCC   +5V (100nF to GND, right at the chip)
   7   GND   GND

  Keep Y1, R1, R2 and C1 tight against U7 with short leads; keep pin 8 -> U6 pin 2 the
  shortest wire on the board.

U6 74LS161 - clock divider
  Pin  Name  Connects to
   1   /CLR  +5V
   2   CLK   U7 8 (10 MHz)
   3   A     GND
   4   B     GND
   5   C     GND
   6   D     GND
   7   ENP   +5V
   8   GND   GND
   9   /LOAD +5V
  10   ENT   +5V
  11   QD    n/c      (625 kHz = 10 MHz / 16, the slower option)
  12   QC    U1 37    (CPU clock: 1.25 MHz = 10 MHz / 8)
  13   QB    n/c
  14   QA    n/c
  15   RCO   n/c
  16   VCC   +5V (100nF to GND)

  CHECK: DC volts at U7 8 = about 1.5-2.5 V (oscillating); pull the crystal out and it stops.

=================================================================================
STAGE 5 - ADDRESS DECODER 74LS00 (U4)
=================================================================================
  /ROMCS = NAND(A15, A14)     low for $C000-$FFFF      -> EEPROM /CE
  /IOSEL = NAND(A15, /ROMCS)  low for $8000-$BFFF      -> LCD latch /LOAD
  WR     = NAND(R/W, R/W)     = NOT R/W, low on reads  -> EEPROM /OE, SRAM /OE
  /WSTB  = NAND(phi2, WR)     low in phi2 of a write   -> SRAM /WE, LCD latch CLK

  Pin  Name        Connects to
   1   1A          U1 25 (A15)
   2   1B          U1 24 (A14)
   3   1Y /ROMCS   U2 20, U4 5
   4   2A          U1 25 (A15)
   5   2B          U4 3
   6   2Y /IOSEL   U5 9
   7   GND         GND
   8   3Y WR       U2 22, U3 22, U4 13
   9   3A          U1 34 (R/W)
  10   3B          U1 34 (R/W)
  11   4Y /WSTB    U3 27, U5 2 (via a 10pF to GND at U5 2: stage 7)
  12   4A          U1 39 (phi2)
  13   4B          U4 8
  14   VCC         +5V (100nF to GND)

=================================================================================
STAGE 7 - LCD LATCH 74LS161 (U5)  at $8000
=================================================================================
  Used as a 4-bit register, not a counter: /LOAD is held low while the CPU writes
  $8000-$BFFF, and the write strobe clocks D0-D3 in.
  Bits: bit0 SDA, bit1 SCL, bit2 DC, bit3 CS.

  Pin  Name   Connects to
   1   /CLR   U1 40 (/RES)          (all LCD lines low during reset)
   2   CLK    U4 11 (/WSTB), and C4 10pF to GND right at this pin (stops ringing)
   3   A      U1 33 (D0)
   4   B      U1 32 (D1)
   5   C      U1 31 (D2)
   6   D      U1 30 (D3)
   7   ENP    GND
   8   GND    GND
   9   /LOAD  U4 6 (/IOSEL)
  10   ENT    GND
  11   QD     LCD CS
  12   QC     LCD DC
  13   QB     LCD SCL
  14   QA     LCD SDA
  15   RCO    n/c
  16   VCC    +5V (100nF to GND)


=================================================================================
STAGE 8 - LCD MODULE (J1)  ST7789, 320x240
=================================================================================
  RULE: never feed an LCD input 5 V, and every LCD input is low while the module powers
  up. NO pull-ups, NO series resistors, NO dividers on any LCD pin.

  LCD pin  Connects to                  Level
  VCC      +5V (10uF + 100nF at module)  module makes its own 3.3 V
  GND      GND
  SDA      U5 14 (QA), direct            0 / about 3.4 V
  SCL      U5 13 (QB), direct
  DC       U5 12 (QC), direct
  CS       U5 11 (QD), direct
  RST      U7 4                          low during CPU reset, high after
  EN       U7 4 (same wire as RST)
  TE       n/c


=================================================================================
STAGE 9 - BUTTONS AND RESET
=================================================================================

Reset (SW1) and power-on reset, at U1 40:
  U1 40 -- R7 10k -- +5V
  U1 40 -- C2 10uF -- GND   (+ side to pin 40; about 100 ms power-on reset)
  U1 40 -- SW1 -- GND       (4-leg button: use two diagonally opposite legs)

LCD reset buffer (U7 74LS04 gates 1, 2), so the LCD's RST/EN follow /RES:
  Pin  Name  Connects to
   1   1A    U1 40 (/RES)
   2   1Y    U7 3
   3   2A    U7 2
   4   2Y    LCD RST and LCD EN
   5   3A    GND (unused gate: input tied low)
   6   3Y    n/c

Flap button (SW2) on /NMI, at U1 6:
  U1 6 -- R5 3.3k -- +5V            (the pull-up, from stage 3)
  U1 6 -- one wire -- BUTTON NODE   (a free breadboard strip next to the button)
  BUTTON NODE -- C3 100nF -- GND    (debounce)
  BUTTON NODE -- 100R -- SW2 leg A
  SW2 leg B -- GND

  CHECK: U1 6 reads about 5 V released and about 0 V held down. If it is low when
  released, the button legs are the wrong pair: turn the button 90 degrees.

PASSIVE PARTS
=================================================================================
  R1, R2   560R    oscillator feedback (U7 13-12, U7 11-10)
  C1       100nF   oscillator coupling (U7 12 - U7 11)
  R3-R6    3.3k    pull-ups: RDY, /IRQ, /NMI, SO
  R7       10k     /RES pull-up
  C2       10uF    power-on reset (U1 40 to GND)
  C3       100nF   flap button debounce
  100R             flap button series resistor
  C4       10pF    latch clock (U5 2) to GND
  C5, C8   10uF    +5V bulk
  C6       100nF   +5V at the input
  C7       470uF   +5V bulk at the adapter
  100nF x7         one per chip, VCC to GND
  10uF + 100nF     at the LCD module
